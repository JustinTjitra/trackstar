UI mockups

Static mockups of the new browser UI. The dark gradient stands in for your camera feed. In every option the dashed line marks the deck split: a hand on the left half controls deck A, on the right half deck B.

1. Where deck state lives

The ring around each hand (section 2) is the same in all three. What differs is where loop and stem state sits. Each frame shows deck A with a 2-beat loop on and vocals muted, and deck B arming.

2. Clutch feedback on the hand

The ring follows your hand, so you never look away from it to know whether you are armed. This is one action from start to finish, which is why it is numbered.

3. Control states

Every loop and stem chip can be in one of these states. Pending and no-confirmation come from the feedback rule: state only changes when rekordbox says so.

What I need from you

  1. Pick a layout: A, B or C (or a mix, for example B with A's vertical stacking).
  2. Does the ring sequence read clearly, or is anything there unclear or too busy?
  3. Stems: lit means audible and struck-through means muted. Does that match how you think about it, or would you rather lit meant muted?
